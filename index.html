Why an entire society for one kind of cake?

Sponge cake is the oldest trick in baking: just eggs, sugar, flour, and enough whipped-in air to make something dense feel like it's floating. We think that's worth celebrating properly: test batches, tasting nights, and way too many opinions about crumb structure.

Everything on this page is our own invention: original mascot, original recipes, original bad puns. No borrowed characters, just a cartoon sponge with a spatula and strong opinions about genoise.

One flavor, done properly

We stopped chasing novelty and picked a single flavor to get exactly right: vanilla, in every form it can take.

Classic Vanilla Sponge

Whipped-egg base, real vanilla bean, barely sweetened so the crumb speaks for itself.

Health rating: 3.5 / 5

Ingredients

  • 4 large eggs
  • 200g caster sugar
  • 200g plain flour
  • 1 vanilla bean (or 2 tsp vanilla extract)
  • 100g unsalted butter, melted
  • 60ml whole milk
  • 1.5 tsp baking powder
  • Pinch of salt
